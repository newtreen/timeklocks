# Time Klocks — Privacy Policy

*Effective: 2026*

Time Klocks does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

- **No analytics.** The app contains no analytics, tracking, or
  advertising frameworks.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for.
- **Your settings stay on your Mac.** Clock configurations, formats, and
  preferences are stored locally in the app's sandboxed container and
  never leave your machine.
- **One network request.** The app periodically downloads the public
  leap-second list from `ietf.org` (a plain text file) to keep the
  GPS−UTC offset current. This request contains no personal information.
- **Purchases** are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.
  Time Klocks never sees your payment information.
- **Imported files** (such as SCLK kernels or images) are read once and
  stored only within the app's local settings.

If this policy ever changes, the change will be noted here with a new
effective date.
